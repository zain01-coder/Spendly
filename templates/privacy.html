Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Spendly personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address, when you register.
  • Expense data you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic usage information, such as login times, needed to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to:

  • Operate core features of the Service, such as logging and displaying your expenses.
  • Generate summaries, category breakdowns, and spending insights for your account.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Service.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and are only accessible to you through your authenticated account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your data at any time by removing your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) solely to operate the Service, and these providers only process data as needed to keep the Service running.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the support channels provided within the application.
